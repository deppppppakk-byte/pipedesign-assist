# Privacy Policy for LumaSnap – Offline Camera

**Effective date:** 15 August 2026  
**Package name:** `com.dpk.lumasnap`

LumaSnap – Offline Camera is designed to work completely offline and to keep user media on the device. The app does not request Android's INTERNET permission and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud backup, or a user-account system.

## Information handled by LumaSnap

LumaSnap may handle camera images, videos, optional microphone audio, local media-file information, app settings, and—only when enabled by the user—device location for photo coordinates. Processing takes place locally on the user's device.

LumaSnap does not transmit this information to the developer or to a developer-controlled server.

## Photos and videos

Photos and videos are created only when the user chooses to capture media. Ordinary captures are stored locally on the device using Android's media-storage system. LumaSnap does not automatically upload photos or videos.

Users control the deletion, editing, movement, and sharing of their media.

## Permissions

LumaSnap may request the following Android permissions when required for a feature:

- **Camera:** used to display the camera preview and capture photos or videos.
- **Microphone:** used only for recording audio with video when the user chooses to record video with sound.
- **Photos, videos, or storage:** used only where required by the Android version to read or write LumaSnap media stored on the device.
- **Location:** optional and used only when the user enables location tagging. When enabled, an available device location may be added to a photograph's metadata.
- **Vibration:** used for local device feedback where supported.

Denying a permission does not cause LumaSnap to send data elsewhere. Features that require a denied permission may remain unavailable.

## Sharing and metadata

LumaSnap shares media only after the user chooses a sharing action and selects a destination through Android's sharing interface. Any third-party app chosen by the user as a sharing destination operates under its own privacy policy.

Where available, LumaSnap may provide an option to remove metadata before sharing. This processing occurs locally on the device.

## Private storage and security

Where LumaSnap provides private or protected local storage features, files remain on the device and are protected using Android platform security mechanisms. Users are responsible for maintaining device access controls and backups of irreplaceable media.

No software can guarantee absolute security against a compromised operating system, unauthorized physical access, or malicious software with elevated privileges.

## Data retention and deletion

LumaSnap does not maintain a remote database of user photos, videos, recordings, locations, or account information.

Media stored on the device remains there until the user deletes it or it is removed by an Android system action or another app with appropriate permission. App-local information can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, subject to Android's storage behavior.

## Children

LumaSnap does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults through developer-controlled servers because the app is designed without network-based collection of user data.

## Advertising, analytics, and tracking

LumaSnap does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or behavioral tracking systems in this offline release.

## Third-party services

LumaSnap relies on Android operating-system services for functions such as camera access, media storage, permissions, device authentication, and the system sharing interface. Those platform services are governed by the policies applicable to the user's Android device and service provider.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if LumaSnap's features or data practices change. If a future version introduces network-dependent functionality or new data handling, this policy will be revised accordingly.
